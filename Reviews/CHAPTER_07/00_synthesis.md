## STORY LOGIC

**SL-1 — Healer's diagnostic mechanism is unearned (critical)**
Source: Story Analyst
> "She says your Trait registration is empty. That sentence altered the room at once."

Fix: The healer's ability to read system registration through touch has been invented purely to serve this scene. Either establish earlier — on the slope, or at the waystation door — that healers conduct a basic registration scan as part of triage (one line from Keiran, or a pause during initial wound assessment that Adrian wonders about), or deliver the revelation through a different mechanism that has been seeded (the system surfacing the information in response to a new social context; another character asking Adrian about his trait as part of assessing him). The dramatic beat is correct. The delivery mechanism needs to be earned before it fires.
- [x]

---

**SL-2 — The Hunt event arrives without situational preparation (critical)**
Source: Story Analyst | Royal Road Reader
> "[System Event Pending] [The Hunt]"

Fix: A thirty-day sealed predator event overriding a monitored first-ranging route has no prior seeding of the conditions that make it possible or likely. "The system did it" is not a story-logic explanation. Seed one prior detail — a line from Keiran about why waystation routes are monitored, or a moment where the big man checks something about their location that a reader can retroactively understand as event-boundary awareness. Even one sentence makes this feel like consequence rather than authorial intervention.
- [x]

**SL-3 — Adrian's 4-day knowledge of Empty — unexplained incuriosity**
Source: Story Analyst
> "He had known the word Empty since the system first showed it to him four days ago. Up to now he had treated it like a technical failure, an ugly blank in the interface where some seeded second layer ought to have been."

Fix: A careful, observant man who asks questions when he has the opportunity ignored a conspicuous system anomaly for four days until a healer forced it public. Either have him acknowledge deliberate avoidance (establish why — denial, survival priority, reluctance to draw attention to something that might mark him as anomalous), or have him already know it is a social problem and have been actively managing that knowledge. Either version is plausible; the current version is a gap.
- [x]

**SL-4 — Skill acquisition timing inconsistent with stated mechanic**
Source: Story Analyst
> "It was not knowledge he had earned enough to trust, only a thin extra layer of certainty dropped onto the work in front of him." / `New Skill Acquired: [Cook — Inferior]`

Fix: The chapter establishes that skill recognition requires pattern, repetition, and honest engagement — then shows it firing on a single, failed attempt. The Inferior grade softens this but does not close the gap. Either have the system explanation come before the event so the rule is in place, or give Adrian a brief beat recognising he has been doing something analogous unconsciously long enough to constitute a pattern (managing the fire, sharpening the knife, arranging the cuts) before the notification arrives.
- [x]

**SL-5 — Chapter ending lacks forward action given established urgency**
Source: Story Analyst | Lector
> "Keiran had not moved much, which was exactly why Adrian watched him harder... Bad enough that the next twelve hours matter."

Fix: Keiran has told him the next twelve hours matter. Adrian ends the chapter sitting by the fire, turning things over in his head. A man who consistently moves toward information under pressure should be beginning to act on this — a question to Keiran, a request from the big man, an attempt to negotiate his position before it crystallises. The chapter does not need to resolve his plan; it needs him to start forming one rather than summarising what he knows.

## CHARACTER GAPS

**CG-1 — Empty Trait revelation costs Adrian nothing visible (critical)**
Source: Character Auditor
> "He pulled the coat back on, slower than he wanted because his ribs objected when he reached too far, then crossed to a stretch of wall."

Fix: This is the chapter's central dramatic event from a character standpoint. The suppression should be a visible effort here, not a foregone conclusion. After the coat goes back on and before he crosses to the wall: one internal beat where the suppression is a visible act rather than natural composure. Not grief, not self-pity — his voice does not do those. Something drier: the recognition that the warmth the room briefly offered was conditional all along, that he knew it was, and that knowing it does not make the withdrawal cleaner.

**CG-2 — The functioning body motivation goes quiet in the healing scene (critical)**
Source: Character Auditor
> "Thirty seconds later the worst of it had eased to something manageable. Whatever else this world turned out to be, his ribs had an opinion on whether he was alive in it."

Fix: The healer is treating his injuries. His body is responding to healing in the direction of recovery. This is the closest the chapter comes to the central payoff of Adrian's existence in this world, and the narrative frames it as proof-of-existence rather than proof that the gamble paid. After the worst of the rib pain eases, one internal beat connecting the healing to the disease-history: the body responding to care is a specific thing his old body could not do in the same direction. He does not have to name the disease. He only has to notice, in one sentence, that this is what he paid for.

## THE WRITING PROFESSIONAL'S QUESTION

**Primary question:**
