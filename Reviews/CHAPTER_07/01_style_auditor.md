## SENTENCE RHYTHM

**Flag 1 — Long analytical sentence during action**

> "The shine on one strip meant rendered fat. The pale bead at the corner of another meant it needed a few breaths longer. Heat was pooling under the left side of the stone. The borrowed knife had made the cuts uneven, and the uneven cuts were dictating everything after that."

Rule: Long analytical sentences (40+ words) should appear only during reasoning and exposition, not during action. Here the opposite problem applies: the analytical reasoning is fragmented into short declarative sentences during an active task (cooking at the hearth with an audience). The rhythm should compress into action syntax, then open into analysis when he sits back. Instead it runs as a flat list of observations with no differentiation between action beat and interpretive beat.

Correction: Merge the observation-pairs into one or two longer analytical sentences that follow the action. "The shine on one strip meant rendered fat, and the pale bead at the corner meant it needed longer — the borrowed knife had made the cuts uneven, and the uneven cuts were running the rest." Then cut to a short action beat.

Flag per: "Flag any stretch of three or more consecutive sentences that are all short (under 10 words) outside a pressure exchange." Counting sentences in the preceding paragraph: "He straightened, rolled one shoulder..." through "It was simply a piece of work..." — four of the seven sentences are under ten words.

## OVERALL COMPLIANCE SCORE: 4 / 10

**Dominant failure pattern:** The chapter's primary failure is structural over-engineering masking as authentic voice. The prose has internalized a set of rhythmic and structural habits — two-line contrast pairs, clean thesis-antithesis-synthesis sequences, negation-reveal constructions, completeness-padding rule-of-three lists, subject-first paragraph openers — and deploys them so consistently that they stop functioning as deliberate choices and become default machinery. The result is a chapter that reads technically competent but tonally uniform: every paragraph feels approximately the same level of finished, which is the exact condition the Anti-AI Tell Lock rules are designed to prevent. The protagonist's analytical voice is present but never rough. His reasoning sequences resolve cleanly rather than self-correcting. Casual intensifiers are entirely absent from narration. The expository passages, particularly the passive-skill explanation and the Empty-processing block, detach from Adrian's specific cognitive texture and deliver clean information in a register that could belong to any litRPG novel. The fix is not local — it requires introducing deliberate unevenness: self-correction moves in the reasoning, rougher diction in the narration, paragraph widening after pressure peaks, and character-specific filtering on every expository passage.
