## THE ONE QUESTION

*From the Writing Professional. Not a fix — a structural decision the author must make before the next draft.*

> **When Adrian's anomalous nature begins to surface through observable events rather than system notifications, what is the cost of his not knowing what he is — and is the author willing to let that cost be real?**
